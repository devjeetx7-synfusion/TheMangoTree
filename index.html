Our story

More than a cafe,
a place to belong.

The Mango Tree Cafe is a warm little corner of Chhapra, Bihar — good food, soft lighting and company that makes an ordinary afternoon feel like an occasion.

Come for a quick coffee, stay for a long conversation. There is room for every craving, every catch-up and every reason to linger a little longer.

  • Freshly prepared food, made to order
  • Cosy ambience, warm lighting and slow evenings
  • Great company — and room for one more chair
